Abstract from OpenAlex (for reference; not shown on the site):
Energy Communities (ECs) help to address energy poverty and sustainability issues. According to Socio-Technical Systems (STS) theory, the success of ECs depends on social motivation. Expanding the attention of literature and policy to ECs necessitates studying motivators, drivers, and barriers for EC participation in order to advance understanding of ECs as STS. In this systematic literature review (SLR) of fifty-two studies, following PRISMA 2020 guidelines, a thematic analysis is conducted based on the ScienceDirect database as of April 25 2025. The findings indicate the importance of financial support, economic gains, a positive environmental attitude, a high sense of community, and the benefits of renewable energy (RE) technologies for EC participation, while financial and regulatory complexity discourages joining ECs. The variety of identified factors underscores the recognition of EC establishment as a dynamic process unique to each region. The results indicate a significant role of socio-technical interaction, government attention, and a need for deeper analysis of cultural and demographic factors for increasing participation.
