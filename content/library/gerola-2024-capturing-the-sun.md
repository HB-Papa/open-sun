The authors treat imagination as a collective capability that can fuel resistance and resilience, but warn that power imbalances can distort it. Looking at two examples, they argue that solarpunk, especially its visual style, can be “captured” by elites in ways that strip out its radical politics, so that even well-meant uses end up making the way things are seem normal.

**Why it matters:** A sharp warning about how hopeful imagery can be borrowed to sell the very systems solarpunk criticizes.
