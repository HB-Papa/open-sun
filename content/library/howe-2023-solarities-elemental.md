An essay collection about “solarity,” our relationship to the sun and its force on life. Drawing on Black ecologies, Indigenous philosophy, feminist studies of science, and thinking that centers other living things as well as people, the essays ask how the sun shapes bodies and lives now and in the future, and what changes when we stop taking it for granted.

**Why it matters:** Widens solar thinking beyond panels and kilowatt-hours to the sun as a shared condition of life.
