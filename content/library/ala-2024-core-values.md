Adopted by the ALA Council in January 2024, these core values guide library workers in the United States: access, equity, intellectual freedom and privacy, the public good, and sustainability. The sustainability value calls for choices that are good for the environment, economically sensible, and equitable, and for libraries to support climate resilience in their communities.

**Why it matters:** Shows that sustainability is now formally part of what U.S. libraries stand for.
