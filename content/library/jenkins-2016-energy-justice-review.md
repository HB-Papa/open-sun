This review sets out energy justice as a framework built on three tenets: distributional justice (who gets the benefits and burdens), recognition justice (who is overlooked or misrepresented), and procedural justice (who gets a say). It proposes a research agenda for applying the framework to energy systems.

**Why it matters:** The standard starting point for talking about fairness in energy.
