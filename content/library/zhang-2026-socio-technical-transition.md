Torrefaction gently heats plant material, or biomass, to upgrade it into a better solid fuel. This review gathers recent research on the technology and builds a framework for judging whether it could make energy more available, accessible, affordable and acceptable, and weighs its fairness, including who gains or loses and who has a say. The authors conclude that technical promise is not enough, and they call for active governance tied to fairness, strong sustainability certification and business models centered on communities.

**Why it matters:** Argues that even a renewable fuel needs fair rules and community stakes to belong in a just energy future.
