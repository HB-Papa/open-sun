Surveying 415 library directors in 13 Midwestern states, the authors found that public libraries help people find jobs and social services, serve as daytime shelters during extreme heat and cold, and adapted quickly during COVID-19, for example by lending Wi-Fi hotspots. These services held regardless of a library’s resources, and the authors urge planners to work with libraries on disaster preparedness.

**Why it matters:** Hard evidence that libraries already act as resilience hubs, often without being called that.
