Opening with her own experience of a storm with nowhere to go, Jarrell-King argues that Connecticut needs shelters people can rely on before, during, and after extreme weather. She points to resilience hubs in California, Baltimore, and Hawai‘i, notes that Connecticut’s first hubs are being built by community groups such as Hartford’s Swift Factory, and lists ways residents can push for more.

**Why it matters:** A local, personal case for resilience hubs, including libraries as partners.
