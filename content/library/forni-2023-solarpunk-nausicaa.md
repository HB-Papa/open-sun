Forni introduces solarpunk and reads Hayao Miyazaki’s 1984 animated film Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind through its lens, since the film shares much of the movement’s outlook on sustainability. She concludes that solarpunk stories, or solarpunk readings of other stories, can help young people understand climate change and feel called to environmental responsibility.

**Why it matters:** Shows how a beloved film can open solarpunk ideas to children and teenagers.
