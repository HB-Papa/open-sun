The Sustainable Library was a Danish public library sharing initiative that ran from 2016 to 2022, an early example of a library lending things and focusing explicitly on sustainability. Looking back over its life cycle, the authors ask how and why it was developed, what drove it, which barriers it met, and what role public libraries should play in sustainability.

**Why it matters:** Honest lessons from a library-of-things project that has run its full course.
