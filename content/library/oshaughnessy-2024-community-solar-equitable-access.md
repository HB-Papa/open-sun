Rooftop solar is out of reach for many renters, apartment dwellers, and lower-income households. Using data from 11 states, the authors found that in 2023 community solar subscribers were about 6.1 times more likely than rooftop adopters to live in multifamily buildings, 4.4 times more likely to rent, and earned 23% less, with business models and policy each driving about half of that difference.

**Why it matters:** Evidence that shared solar really does reach people rooftop solar leaves out. The free version is the authors’ preprint.
