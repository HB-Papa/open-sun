Using detailed laser-mapping (LiDAR) data for 128 U.S. cities, NREL analysts estimated that suitable rooftops across the country could host 1,118 gigawatts of solar panels and generate 1,432 terawatt-hours a year, equal to about 39% of national electricity sales. That nearly doubled the lab’s earlier estimate.

**Why it matters:** Shows how much clean power is waiting on roofs we already have, no new land needed.
