Abstract from OpenAlex (for reference; not shown on the site):
Europe’s path to climate neutrality is assessed through the competing lenses of green growth and degrowth. The chapter synthesises theoretical debates, empirical evidence and EU policy initiatives such as the European Green Deal and Fit for 55. Green growth appears more compatible with existing institutions, market systems and political feasibility, yet evidence on absolute decoupling raises doubts about whether it can deliver climate stability at sufficient speed. Degrowth offers a sharper ecological critique and stresses social justice, consumption restraint, and redistribution; however, it faces serious barriers linked to financial systems, public acceptance, employment and EU cohesion. The chapter argues for a hybrid transformation that goes beyond conventional growth while avoiding uncontrolled contraction. Europe may need policies that combine technological change, fairness and deeper institutional redesign.
