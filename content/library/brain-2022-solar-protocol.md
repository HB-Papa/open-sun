Solar Protocol is a website hosted on a network of solar-powered servers looked after by volunteers around the world. Instead of staying on one machine, traffic goes to whichever server has the most sunshine at that moment, letting sunlight decide where computing happens. The authors use the project to sketch six principles of “energy-centered design.”

**Why it matters:** A working example of internet infrastructure that follows the sun, like the Microgrid game does.
