A collection of 13 papers from the 2023 Solarpunk Conference, a free online event, together with papers from other applicants. Written around the conference theme “Imagine, Act, Thrive,” the papers come from both scholars and practitioners, and the contributors are listed alphabetically to show that every contribution is valued equally.

**Why it matters:** A snapshot of the solarpunk community’s own conversations, bridging academic and popular voices.
