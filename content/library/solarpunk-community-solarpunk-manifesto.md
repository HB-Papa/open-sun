A collectively written statement of what solarpunk stands for, adapted from many contributors’ ideas. It rejects dystopian futures in favor of hopeful, renewable-powered ones; pairs beautiful design with ecological care; favors local, decentralized action; welcomes every culture and identity; and treats imaginative fiction as a tool for political and social change.

**Why it matters:** The community’s own description of the movement, and the text many newcomers meet first.
