Rossi uses solarpunk stories as the starting point for “backcasting”: imagining a desirable future city and then working out the steps that could lead there. Drawing on the Nature Futures Framework, climate-resilient development, and multispecies sustainability, the thesis builds hopeful scenarios such as multispecies climate adaptation, social security for animals, and new relationships between species. The results are also shared as a free interactive story.

**Why it matters:** Shows how solarpunk fiction can be turned into a practical planning method, not just inspiration.
