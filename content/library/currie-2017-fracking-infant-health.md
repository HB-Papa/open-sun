Analyzing more than 1.1 million Pennsylvania births from 2004 to 2013, and comparing siblings born before and after fracking began nearby, the authors found harm to babies whose mothers lived within 3 kilometers of active fracking sites, with the largest effects within 1 kilometer. Effects included more low-birth-weight babies and lower average birth weight.

**Why it matters:** Concrete evidence of the human health costs of fossil fuels that a solar future could avoid.
