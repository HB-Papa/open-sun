Ettarh names “vocational awe”: the belief that libraries are inherently good, even sacred, and therefore beyond criticism. She argues this belief is directly linked to problems like burnout and low pay among library workers, and shows how it shapes library work, so that people can spot it and push back.

**Why it matters:** An important caution for anyone celebrating libraries: caring for the commons includes caring for the people who keep it running.
