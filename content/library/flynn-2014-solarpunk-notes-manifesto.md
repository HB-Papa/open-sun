An early, influential essay that helped define solarpunk. Flynn presents it as an optimistic alternative to cyberpunk’s nihilism and steampunk’s nostalgia, built on ingenuity, independence, and community. He treats infrastructure as a form of resistance and imagines a “smart citizenry” in place of the “smart city.”

**Why it matters:** Short, quotable, and one of the texts most people cite when explaining where solarpunk came from.
