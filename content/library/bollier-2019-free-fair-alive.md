Bollier and Helfrich argue that “commoning,” people organizing together to meet shared needs, is not a failed way of managing resources, as mainstream economics claims, but a living social system. They describe the patterns behind successful commons around the world and present commoning as a practical path to a post-capitalist economy that is free, fair, and ecologically sustainable.

**Why it matters:** The most complete guide to the commons, one of solarpunk’s central ideas, and free to read online.
