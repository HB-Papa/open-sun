Much solarpunk fiction imagines that high technology and industry can simply become green. Rivero-Vadillo reads two short stories that push back, picturing low-tech, degrowth communities living alongside solarpunk spaces. He argues these stories expose the assumptions of techno-optimism and offer alternatives to it.

**Why it matters:** A useful counterweight for readers who worry solarpunk can slide into green consumerism.
