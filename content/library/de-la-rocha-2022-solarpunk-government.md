Many solarpunks call to “smash the state,” but De La Rocha asks whether there is also room for a government whose honest purpose is helping everyone live a good, meaningful life. She sketches what that could mean: ending hunger, homelessness, and poverty, opening education to all, and building and maintaining shared infrastructure, from the power grid and clean water to healthcare and childcare.

**Why it matters:** Opens up the debate for readers who share solarpunk’s goals but aren’t drawn to anarchism.
