This review explains degrowth: the idea that the economy should be radically reorganized to use fewer resources and less energy, and that such a shift is necessary, desirable, and possible. It surveys research on economic stability without growth, societies that have done well without it, and the kinds of technology and democracy that fit a degrowth transition.

**Why it matters:** The clearest scholarly overview of degrowth, an idea close to the heart of solarpunk.
