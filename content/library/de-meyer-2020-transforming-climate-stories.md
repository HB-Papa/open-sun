Most environmental news is negative, on the assumption that awareness and fear must come before action. Reviewing research on the mind and brain, the authors argue the opposite is often true: our actions shape our beliefs. They propose telling many stories of people taking positive climate action, as citizens, professionals, and community members, so that people feel they can act too.

**Why it matters:** Evidence for why hopeful, action-focused stories like solarpunk can matter.
