Researchers working with IPBES, the global science-policy body on biodiversity, developed the Nature Futures Framework: a triangle that captures diverse, positive ways people relate to nature. The paper explains how it was built and argues it can hold many views of what is desirable while helping build consistent scenarios for nature from local to global scales.

**Why it matters:** A tool that turns hopeful visions of nature into scenarios that governments can actually use.
