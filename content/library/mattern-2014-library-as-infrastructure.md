Public libraries now serve as reading rooms, social service centers, and innovation labs. Mattern asks how far that can stretch, and argues for seeing libraries as interconnected infrastructures: spatial, technological, social, and intellectual. She traces the idea back to Melvil Dewey and the industrial-era library.

**Why it matters:** A classic essay for understanding why libraries, like this one, are such powerful shared infrastructure.
