The climate crisis is landing unequally: a “climate privileged” group, mostly in the Global North, can protect itself, while a “climate precariat,” mostly in the Global South, cannot. Wood and Meyer call this a form of climate apartheid and argue for “narrative justice”: stories, especially climate-focused speculative fiction, that challenge this path and offer fairer visions of the global future.

**Why it matters:** A South African perspective on why the stories we tell about climate change are a question of justice.
