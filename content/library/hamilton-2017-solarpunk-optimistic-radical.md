A short explainer for newcomers. Hamilton describes solarpunk as a movement in fiction, art, fashion, and activism asking what a sustainable civilization looks like and how to get there, then asks how such an optimistic movement can still be “punk.” Her answer: by imagining futures that demand radical change to energy, services, and transport, using many technologies and practices that already exist.

**Why it matters:** One of the clearest short introductions, written for a general audience.
