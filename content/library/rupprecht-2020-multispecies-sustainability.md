Sustainability usually means balancing the needs of present and future humans, treating nature only as a resource. The authors argue that true sustainability means meeting the interdependent needs of all species, now and in the future, and call this “multispecies sustainability.” They explore the idea through visualizations and scenarios and apply it to cases involving bees and healthy green spaces.

**Why it matters:** A foundational idea for solarpunk cities, co-written by Jay Springett, an early voice of the solarpunk movement.
