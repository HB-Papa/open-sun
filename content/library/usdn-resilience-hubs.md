Resilience hubs are existing community spaces upgraded to support residents, coordinate communication, and distribute resources before, during, and after a disaster, while also cutting carbon pollution. This guide walks local governments and community groups through making the case, choosing a site, and running a hub, which it says should be led by the community with government support.

**Why it matters:** A practical playbook for turning everyday community spaces into lifelines.
