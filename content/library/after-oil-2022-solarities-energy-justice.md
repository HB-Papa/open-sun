A short collective book on what it would mean to organize societies around solar energy, and on the challenges of a just move away from fossil fuels. Rather than treating solar as a simple fix, activists, scholars, and practitioners look at how solar is built, how small, local solar systems could help undo colonial and patriarchal power, how solar affects other living things, and how solar can be used to cause harm, even as a weapon.

**Why it matters:** A free, readable book that keeps the “justice” in solar futures front and center.
