Okoroafor proposes solarpunk as a way to build a sustainable future for African children. She links its emphasis on community and harmony with nature to African philosophies, reviews the challenges children face, and discusses forest guards and social action groups in protecting biodiversity. The article ends with policy recommendations for adopting solarpunk principles in Africa.

**Why it matters:** Brings children and African philosophy, both often missing from solarpunk debates, to the center.
