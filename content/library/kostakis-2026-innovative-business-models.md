Abstract from OpenAlex (for reference; not shown on the site):
This article introduces the Special Issue “Multi-Level Innovations in A Circular Economy” and situates it within the ongoing debate on how firms can move from linear to circular value creation. We discuss how business model innovation, understood as the redesign of a firm’s value proposition, revenue architecture, and resource base, intersects with business responsibility, corporate social responsibility (CSR), and the broader agenda of responsible innovation, drawing on recent evidence linking circular practices to firm performance across sectors and institutional contexts. We further consider the multi-actor, multi-level nature of the circular transition, spanning international organisations, industries, consumers, SMEs, and local NGOs, and the corresponding micro, meso, and macro dimensions of circular economy policy. This multi-level innovations perspective, spanning the entrepreneurial, community, sectoral, and institutional scales, provides the organising lens for the Special Issue as a whole. The six contributions gathered in this Special Issue examine this transition through complementary lenses, the iterative design and implementation of sustainable business models in SMEs, citizen participation in renewable energy communities, sector-level innovation ecosystems in textiles, hierarchical prioritisation of circular enablers in solid waste management for the steel industry, industrial symbiosis under institutional disruption, and digital information infrastructures for product markets, showing that scaling circular business models beyond isolated pilots depends as much on institutional design and organisational responsibility as on technological readiness. The article closes by outlining the scope of the Special Issue and directions for future research at the interface of circular business models and responsible entrepreneurship.
