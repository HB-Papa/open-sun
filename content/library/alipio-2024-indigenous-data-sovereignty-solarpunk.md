The rapid growth of AI and data centers is driving huge energy use and waste heat. The authors propose policies for earth-friendly computing rooted in Indigenous systems where nothing is wasted, especially the Hawaiian value of aloha ʻāina (love for the land). They pair this traditional knowledge of the land with solarpunk visions of technology that works with the environment: reusing electronic waste, and storing and retiring data more carefully.

**Why it matters:** Brings Indigenous knowledge and solarpunk together on one of today’s most urgent tech questions: the footprint of AI.
