A survey of 10,000 people aged 16 to 25 in ten countries found that 59% were very or extremely worried about climate change, and more than 45% said those feelings affected their daily lives. Distress was linked to a sense that governments were failing to respond, with respondents reporting more betrayal than reassurance.

**Why it matters:** Shows why hopeful, action-oriented futures matter for young people’s wellbeing.
