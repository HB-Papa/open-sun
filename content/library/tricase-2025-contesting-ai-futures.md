As Italy invests heavily in artificial intelligence, grassroots movements are pushing back on the idea that AI is mainly a driver of growth and competitiveness. Through fieldwork, interviews, and activist documents from 2023 to 2024, the authors identify three shared visions, or “imaginaries,” among activists: AI Utopia, AI Dystopia, and “Solarpunk AI,” which pictures technology shaped by justice, sustainability, and democratic participation.

**Why it matters:** Shows solarpunk moving from fiction into real social movements arguing over technology. Note that this is a preprint that has not yet been peer reviewed.
