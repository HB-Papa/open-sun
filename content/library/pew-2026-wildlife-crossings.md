The United States records more than a million collisions between vehicles and wildlife each year, costing over $10 billion and causing about 200 deaths and 26,000 injuries. This fact sheet explains how overpasses, underpasses, and fencing can cut such crashes by more than 80% while reconnecting habitat, and calls for steady funding to build them where collisions are most common.

**Why it matters:** A short, current case for one of the most hopeful forms of multispecies infrastructure.
