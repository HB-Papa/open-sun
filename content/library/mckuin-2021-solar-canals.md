The authors model what would happen if solar panels were built over California’s canals instead of on nearby land. Shading the water cuts evaporation, saving large amounts of water each year, and the combined water and energy benefits outweighed the added cost of building over the canals, giving a better financial return than ground-mounted solar.

**Why it matters:** A clever solarpunk-style idea: generate power without using new land, while saving water.
