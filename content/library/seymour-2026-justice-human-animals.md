Farming without animals, sometimes called veganic or stockfree organic farming, is one path toward climate-friendly food. The authors read the rules of three such farming standards and coded them for four kinds of justice toward animals: recognition, fair sharing of benefits and harms, fair process, and repair of past harm. They draw lessons about which animals deserve moral concern, the role of farmers’ own values and politics, and the pull of the wider food system beyond the farm.

**Why it matters:** Asks what a fair shift to climate-friendly farming would mean for animals, a question close to solarpunk’s hope for a world shared with other species.
