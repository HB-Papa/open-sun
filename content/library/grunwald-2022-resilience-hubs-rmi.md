As climate disasters grow more frequent, dozens of U.S. cities are building resilience hubs: community facilities that support residents, distribute resources, and cut carbon pollution. The authors explain the five areas hubs can cover (reliable power, communication, dependable facilities, operations, and services) and share lessons from cities such as Austin, Texas, that are already deploying them.

**Why it matters:** A practical overview of how cities are turning community buildings into climate lifelines.
