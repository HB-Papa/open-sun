A free anthology of science fiction stories, essays, and art about how solar energy could transform four cities: Chicago, San Juan, Portland, and San Antonio. Stories by Paolo Bacigalupi, S. B. Divya, Andrew Dana Hudson, and Deji Bryce Olukotun are paired with essays by energy researchers.

**Why it matters:** A great first read: hopeful fiction grounded in real places and real energy research.
