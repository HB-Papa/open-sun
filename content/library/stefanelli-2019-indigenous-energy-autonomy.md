This systematic review of Canadian literature from 1980 to 2017 asks why Indigenous peoples lead, partner in, or take part in renewable energy projects. Many are developing renewables in their territories to break colonial ties, move toward energy autonomy, gain more reliable power, and earn long-term income, though the authors caution that more research is needed before treating renewables as a path to reconciliation.

**Why it matters:** Centers Indigenous goals, especially autonomy, in conversations about renewable energy.
