## Purpose and scope

Open Sun is a free, public library of open access writing about solarpunk and the ideas around it. It is an independent, non-commercial project run by Chris Misciagna, MLIS, referred to below as the site owner.

## What we collect

Every item must meet four criteria.

- **Free to read.** The work is open access, which means anyone can read it online at no cost.
- **On topic.** The work is about solarpunk itself or a closely related idea: energy justice and democracy, community solar, degrowth, multispecies and urban ecology, Indigenous and decolonial futures, libraries and the commons, or hopeful, utopian thinking.
- **Trustworthy.** Most items are peer-reviewed scholarship, books from university and nonprofit presses, or practical guides from credible organizations. A preprint is labeled as not yet peer reviewed.
- **Plain language.** Each item gets a short summary and a note on why it matters, written so anyone can follow it.

## Where new items come from

The site owner chose the starting collection by hand. New items arrive in two ways.

- **A weekly search.** Every Monday an automated search checks OpenAlex, the Directory of Open Access Journals and Crossref for newly published open access work. A keyword score screens out results that are off topic.
- **Reader suggestions.** Anyone can suggest a resource through the form on the site.

## How items are reviewed

An AI agent drafts each summary. A second agent, which didn’t write the draft, checks it against the source and against the criteria above. A person approves every item before it is added. Nothing is published automatically.

## Licenses and access

Open Sun links to each work where its publisher or author hosts it. The library doesn’t hold copies. Most items carry a Creative Commons license, and each item’s page shows its license.

## Languages

Most items are in English. The collection also holds a few works in Spanish, Portuguese and Polish. Every summary is written in English, and the summary of a work in another language begins by naming that language.

## Removing items

The site owner removes or corrects an item when:

- its link is dead and no free copy can be found
- it is no longer free to read
- its record or summary has an error that can’t be fixed
- it turns out to be out of scope.

A monthly audit tests every link and every record in the library and posts its report as a public issue on GitHub.

## Corrections and suggestions

To suggest a reading, use the [suggestion form](/suggest). To report a broken link or an error in a summary, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/HB-Papa/open-sun/issues/new), which needs a free GitHub account. Both are public, so please leave out anything private.

## Review of this policy

The site owner updates this policy when the way the library works changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last reviewed.
