---
title: Field Guide
terms:
  - term: Solarpunk
    definition: A movement in fiction, art, design, and activism that imagines a future powered by renewable energy, woven into living ecosystems, and organized around community rather than profit. The name plays on cyberpunk and steampunk, but the mood is hopeful.
    related: [flynn-2014-solarpunk-notes-manifesto, gillam-2023-solarpunk-manifesto, reina-rozo-2021-art-energy-technology]
  - term: Microgrid
    definition: A small, local electricity network, such as a neighborhood or campus, with its own generation and storage. It can share power with the main grid or run on its own when the main grid goes down. It is also the name of Open Sun's daily puzzle.
    related: [szulecki-2018-energy-democracy, usdn-resilience-hubs]
  - term: Agrivoltaics
    definition: Growing crops or grazing animals on the same land as solar panels. The panels give plants shade and the plants keep the panels cooler, so both can do better together.
    related: [barron-gafford-2019-agrivoltaics]
  - term: Energy justice
    definition: Fairness in energy systems. It asks who gets the benefits and burdens (distributional justice), whose needs are recognized (recognition justice), and who gets a say in decisions (procedural justice).
    related: [jenkins-2016-energy-justice-review, sareen-2023-solidaric-solarities, after-oil-2022-solarities-energy-justice]
  - term: Energy democracy
    definition: The idea that people should have real control over the energy that powers their lives, through public or community ownership and participation in decisions.
    related: [szulecki-2018-energy-democracy]
  - term: Prosumer
    definition: Someone who both produces and consumes energy, for example a household with rooftop solar that sends extra power to its neighbors.
    related: [szulecki-2018-energy-democracy]
  - term: Community solar
    definition: A shared solar project that several households or organizations own or subscribe to, so people without a suitable roof can still benefit from solar power.
    related: [sareen-2023-solidaric-solarities, reina-rozo-2024-technologies-embrace-sun]
  - term: Battery storage
    definition: Batteries that save surplus electricity, often from sunny afternoons, so it can be used later, such as after sunset. In the Microgrid puzzle they are what carry sunshine into the night.
    related: [umich-css-2026-us-renewable-energy-factsheet, de-decker-2018-low-tech-website]
  - term: Wind wake
    definition: The slower, choppier air behind a wind turbine. Turbines placed too close together steal wind from each other, which is why the Microgrid puzzle weakens turbines that touch.
    related: []
  - term: Degrowth
    definition: A planned reduction in energy and resource use in wealthy economies, aimed at living well within the planet's limits while reducing inequality.
    related: [kallis-2018-research-on-degrowth, rivero-vadillo-2022-degrowth-imaginaries]
  - term: Commons
    definition: Resources that a community shares and looks after together, such as a library, a park, a seed bank, or open knowledge, instead of resources owned privately or by the state.
    related: [baden-2020-libraries-of-things, de-valk-2021-pluriverse-local-worlds]
  - term: Library of Things
    definition: A lending library for useful objects, such as drills, tents, or sewing machines, so a neighborhood can share them instead of everyone buying their own.
    related: [baden-2020-libraries-of-things]
  - term: Resilience hub
    definition: A trusted community space, often powered by solar and batteries, that supports residents every day and becomes a center for help during disasters.
    related: [usdn-resilience-hubs]
  - term: Multispecies justice
    definition: An approach to justice that counts the needs and flourishing of animals, plants, and ecosystems, not only humans.
    related: [celermajer-2021-multispecies-justice, edwards-2023-more-than-human-counter-cities]
  - term: Wildlife crossing
    definition: A bridge or tunnel that lets animals cross a road safely, usually paired with fencing that guides them to it.
    related: [huijser-2009-wildlife-collisions]
  - term: Solarity
    definition: A word scholars use for our many relationships with the sun, from solar panels to the sunlight that makes all life possible.
    related: [after-oil-2022-solarities-energy-justice, howe-2023-solarities-elemental, williams-2019-shining-confluence]
  - term: Low-tech
    definition: Technology designed to be simple, repairable, and frugal with energy and materials, such as a website built to run on one small solar panel.
    related: [de-decker-2018-low-tech-website, de-valk-2021-pluriverse-local-worlds]
  - term: Speculative fiction
    definition: Stories that ask "what if?" about the future or about other worlds. Solarpunk, climate fiction, and science fiction all belong to this family.
    related: [eschrich-miller-2021-cities-of-light, sinha-2023-speculative-fictions-decolonial, malpas-2021-climate-fiction-vital-tool]
  - term: Indigenous futurisms
    definition: Futures imagined from Indigenous knowledge, experience, and sovereignty, often by Indigenous writers, artists, and communities themselves.
    related: [cheok-2025-indigenous-futures-thinking, reina-rozo-2024-technologies-embrace-sun]
