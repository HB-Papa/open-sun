Open Sun is a free library of open access writing about solarpunk and the ideas around it: solar power and energy justice, living land and multispecies cities, the commons, and hopeful futures. Everything here is free to read.

## What is solarpunk?

Solarpunk imagines a future powered by the sun, woven into living ecosystems, and built around community instead of profit. It began as an internet word around 2008 and has grown into fiction, art, design, scholarship, and activism.

## How items are chosen

- **Free to read.** Every item is open access: free to read online, usually under a Creative Commons license. Each item's page shows its license.
- **On topic.** Items are about solarpunk itself or closely related ideas: energy justice and democracy, community solar, degrowth, multispecies and urban ecology, Indigenous and decolonial futures, libraries and the commons, and hopeful, utopian thinking.
- **Trustworthy.** Most items are peer-reviewed scholarship, books from university and nonprofit presses, or practical guides from credible organizations.
- **Plain language.** Each item has a short summary and a note on why it matters, written to be read by anyone.

## How new items are found

Once a week, an automated search checks three open scholarly databases (OpenAlex, the Directory of Open Access Journals, and Crossref) for newly published open access work on these topics. Anything promising is added to a review list. **A person reads every suggestion and edits every summary before it appears here.** Nothing is published automatically.

You can also [suggest a resource](/suggest).

## The daily puzzle

Microgrid is a short daily puzzle about keeping a small solar neighborhood lit for 24 hours. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Reading in the library earns field notes, which unlock bonus grids and rest days for your streak, but your daily result is always just your own play.

Your progress is saved only in your own browser. Open Sun has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking cookies.

## Accessibility

Open Sun aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, respects your device's "reduce motion" setting, and never uses color as the only signal. The puzzle describes every result in words as well as pictures. If something doesn't work for you, please [tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/HB-Papa/open-sun/issues/new) (a free account is needed) and we'll fix it.
